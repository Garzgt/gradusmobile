# Beautiful UI Guide For Landing Page

This guide keeps the landing page premium, intentional, and not generic.

## 1. Visual Direction

1. Lead with institutional confidence using maroon and gold hierarchy.
2. Pair strong typography with calm spacing.
3. Use expressive but clean section transitions.
4. Keep each section visually distinct while still cohesive.

## 2. Typography Strategy

1. Hero headline should feel bold and memorable.
2. Body text should stay highly readable for long scanning.
3. Section titles should be short and benefit-oriented.
4. Keep line lengths comfortable on desktop and mobile.

## 3. Composition Rules

1. Alternate dense and airy sections to avoid visual fatigue.
2. Use asymmetric layouts in selected sections for character.
3. Keep CTAs in predictable positions for fast decisions.
4. Use visual anchors such as badges and chips to guide scanning.

## 4. Motion Language

1. Use short reveal animations for section entry.
2. Use timeline and card motion to explain relationships.
3. Avoid decorative motion loops that distract from CTA actions.
4. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## 5. Color and Contrast Rules

1. Maroon is for authority and primary conversion points.
2. Gold is for highlights, not full-page saturation.
3. Keep large backgrounds neutral for readability.
4. Ensure contrast remains accessible for every action state.

## 6. Public Achievers Visual Style

1. Highlight top three with distinct badge treatment.
2. Keep ranking table clean and easy to compare.
3. Use term and program filters as part of the visual hierarchy.
4. Show timestamp and criteria link as trust signals.

## 7. Anti-Patterns To Avoid

1. Do not overload one section with too many cards.
2. Do not use more than one primary CTA color family.
3. Do not rely on color alone for status meaning.
4. Do not use heavy shadows and noisy gradients everywhere.

## 8. Quality Standard

Landing page should feel:
1. Trustworthy
2. Fast
3. Clear
4. Institutional
5. Modern without looking templated

## 9. Image Density Strategy

1. Target total page image count: 48 to 72 (including section visuals and team photos).
2. Keep first-view image count controlled:
- Mobile: 3 to 5
- Tablet: 5 to 8
- Desktop: 8 to 12
3. Use lazy loading for all non-critical sections to keep performance strong.
4. Mix product screenshots and real campus or team visuals for authenticity.

## 10. Developer Team Visual Style

1. Use a formal portrait system with consistent framing and attire.
2. Keep portrait ratio consistent (recommended 4:5).
3. Show role and specialization chips for quick scanning.
4. Highlight leadership members with subtle visual distinction, not loud effects.
5. Avoid mixed low-quality sources that break trust.

## 11. AI Illustration Usage

1. AI-generated cartoon characters are allowed for explanatory visuals.
2. Good usage examples:
- Teacher or student using app mockups in hero or feature explainers.
- Timeline and role cards that benefit from friendly illustration style.
3. Do not use AI cartoons for:
- Developer Team formal profiles
- Public Academic Achievers profiles
- Real testimonials that represent actual people
- Proof visuals for governance, audit, or institutional claims
4. Keep visual trust by mixing media types:
- AI illustrations: 20% to 35%
- Real screenshots and real photos: 65% to 80%
5. Keep one consistent illustration style family across the page.
6. Add a short disclosure note that some illustrations are AI-generated.
