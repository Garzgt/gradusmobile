# Landing Image And Developer Team Guide

This guide answers image quantity targets and defines the Developer Team section for the landing page.

## 1. Direct Answer: How Many Images Should Be Visible

Recommended image density:

1. Total images across full landing page:
- Minimum: 36
- Recommended: 48 to 72
- Maximum without redesign review: 84

2. Images visible at first screen load:
- Mobile: 3 to 5
- Tablet: 5 to 8
- Desktop: 8 to 12

3. Images visible at one time while scrolling deep sections:
- Mobile: 4 to 8
- Tablet: 8 to 12
- Desktop: 10 to 18

Performance guardrail:
- Show many images visually, but lazy-load all non-critical media below the first viewport.

## 2. Recommended Image Distribution By Section

1. Hero section:
- 2 to 4 visual assets

2. Platform access cards:
- 3 assets (one per platform)

3. Timeline and module showcase:
- 8 to 14 assets combined

4. Public achievers and testimonials:
- 6 to 12 assets combined

5. Developer Team section:
- 8 to 24 formal portrait photos

## 3. Developer Team Section Blueprint

Required content per member card:

1. Formal photo
2. Full name
3. Role title (for example: Full Stack Developer)
4. Team focus tag (Frontend, Backend, Mobile, QA, Design)
5. Short contribution line
6. Optional profile links (GitHub, LinkedIn, portfolio)

Suggested grouping:

1. Core leadership
2. Development team
3. QA and support team

## 4. Formal Photo Standards

1. Ratio: 4:5 portrait
2. Recommended size: 1200x1500 minimum
3. Background: clean neutral or campus-consistent style
4. Framing: chest-up composition, clear face visibility
5. Dress code: formal attire
6. Lighting: even and soft, avoid strong shadows

## 5. Team Section UX Rules

1. Grid layout:
- Mobile: 1 to 2 columns
- Tablet: 2 to 3 columns
- Desktop: 4 to 6 columns

2. Interaction:
- Card hover lift on desktop
- Tap-to-open profile modal on mobile
- Keyboard focus and accessible labels required

3. Content safety:
- Use approved names and photos only
- Keep profile copy professional and concise

## 6. UI Target: Surpass Baseline Institutional Pages

To exceed basic institutional landing pages:

1. Use a richer visual story with real product screenshots and people photos.
2. Keep typography hierarchy strong and modern.
3. Use intentional section transitions and premium spacing rhythm.
4. Keep CTA visibility consistent from top to bottom.
5. Balance image richness with fast load through compression and lazy loading.

## 7. Launch Criteria For Image-Heavy Landing

1. First meaningful visual appears quickly on mobile data.
2. No layout shift from late-loading images.
3. Team photos are consistent in crop and quality.
4. All non-decorative images have meaningful alt text.
5. Lighthouse performance remains acceptable after media integration.
